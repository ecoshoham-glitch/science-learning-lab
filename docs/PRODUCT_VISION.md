# Product vision

Science Learning Lab is a standalone, multilingual platform where Israeli high school students
(primarily grades 10–12) explore science through interactive simulations, virtual labs, 3D models and
inquiry activities, and where teachers create, adapt and share these experiences without programming.

- One platform, many independent scientific simulations, multiple activities per simulation.
- Hebrew first, English from day one, more languages later.
- Scientific accuracy, curriculum alignment (verified against official documents), accessibility,
  privacy for minors, and sustainable operating costs.
- Bridges physical educational kits and digital learning (QR now; NFC and AR later).
- AI assists teachers (activities, lessons from transcripts, new simulations) and later students (tutor),
  always with validation and human approval.

The authoritative requirements are the Project Instructions (v2.0) in the Claude Project.
